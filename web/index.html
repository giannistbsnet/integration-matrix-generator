? How matrix mirroring works

Fill the lower-left triangle only. The upper-right triangle mirrors automatically:

  • ↗ mirrors as ↙
  • ↙ mirrors as ↗
  • ↔ mirrors unchanged
  • Interface type copies the counterpart cell

Direction options: ↔ ↗ ↙
